THE RULEBOOK

The fine print,
in plain English.

Who can join?+

Anyone. It's free and takes about 30 seconds with your phone number. Beer wall rewards are for guests of legal drinking age.

How do I earn points?+

10 points for every $1 you spend on bay time, food and the beer wall. Everyone earns on their own account, on what they spend.

How does the Crew Bonus work?+

When 4 or more members play in the same bay, everyone in that bay earns 1.5x points for the night.

How do referrals count?+

When a friend joins through the booking link you shared in the group chat, you each get 250 points. That's the Call-Up Bonus.

Do my points expire?+

Never, as long as you visit at least once a year.

When can I use bay-time rewards?+

Sunday through Thursday. That covers 30 free minutes, a free hour and Crew Night.

How do I move up a tier?+

Tiers are based on visits per year. Everyone starts as a Rookie. Hit 5 visits to become a Starter, 12 to become Franchise.

What if I miss my visit count?+

Soft landing: you drop at most one tier per year. A Franchise member who slows down becomes a Starter, never straight back to Rookie.

When do tiers reset?+

Your visit count resets once a year, on [RESET DATE]. Your tier is then set by last year's visits, with the soft landing applied.

What are surprise drops?+

Every so often a free round pops up on your bay screen. You can't earn them or chase them. They just happen. Thanks for coming back.
